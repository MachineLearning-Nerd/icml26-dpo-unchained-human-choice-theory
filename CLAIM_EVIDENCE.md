# Claim-to-evidence map

This repository separates the paper's theorem contracts from the programs,
raw outputs, independent checkers, controls, and limitations that produce each
status. The production graph is:

    paper anchor -> explicit contract -> independent producer
                 -> raw output -> checker/control -> status and limitation

The fixed cumulative entrypoint is reproduction/run.py. Its released,
machine-readable result is space_candidate/artifacts/results.json. The dossier
cleanup did not rerun the historical scientific campaign.

## C1 — universal proper-composite construction

- Paper anchor: Theorem 4.3, content/generalization.tex:66-71 and
  content/appendix-proofs.tex:125-170.
- Contract: for every strictly increasing psi:R->R and strictly increasing
  F_tilde:R->[0,1], a finite real-valued strictly proper binary loss exists
  with psi(z)=l0(F_tilde(z)) for every real z.
- Producer: reproduction/falsification.py::claim_1_counterexample, with the
  witness psi(z)=z and F(z)=sigmoid(z).
- Evidence: .openresearch/artifacts/claim_1/raw.json,
  independent_checker_output.json, and negative_control_output.json.
- Result: the endpoint contradiction is UNSAT under the properness inequality,
  five high-precision decimal checks agree, the Brier positive control has
  residual 0, and reversing the inequality is SAT.
- Status: **FALSIFIED** for the finite real-valued closed-simplex contract.
- Boundary: extended-real endpoint losses would be a different theorem and are
  not silently substituted.

## C2 — proper-loss regret and Bregman divergence

- Paper anchor: Theorem 4.1, content/generalization.tex:23-29 and
  content/appendix-proofs.tex:4-18.
- Contract: the regret of every proper loss on the simplex equals the
  Bregman divergence generated by its Bayes risk, with the stated subgradient
  selection.
- Producer: reproduction/proof_certificates.py::claim_2_certificate.
- Evidence: .openresearch/artifacts/claim_2/raw.json,
  independent_checker_output.json, and negative_control_output.json.
- Result: symbolic residual 0; 200 exact rational trials across dimensions
  2 through 9 with seed 4201; the wrong-sign control has residual 4.
- Status: **VERIFIED** within the stated proper-loss and simplex scope.
- Boundary: this does not claim that an arbitrary regularizer is a proper-loss
  regret.

## C3 — KLST* and abstention

- Paper anchor: Definitions 3.2–3.4, content/normative-framework.tex:48-83.
- Contract: the stated expandability, local choice, and monotonicity axioms
  define KLST* while permitting abstention through nonedges on the atomic
  domain.
- Producer: reproduction/falsification.py::claim_3_counterexample.
- Evidence: .openresearch/artifacts/claim_3/raw.json,
  independent_checker_output.json, and negative_control_output.json.
- Result: symbolic expansion factors as
  alpha*(alpha - 1)*(1 - choice_sum). For nontrivial alpha, SMT finds
  abstention under all stated axioms UNSAT; dropping mixed-lottery bearability
  makes the abstaining control SAT.
- Status: **FALSIFIED** as an abstention-permitting definition on the stated
  atomic domain.
- Boundary: the result does not automatically falsify every surrounding
  choice-theory result or a repaired definition.

## C4 — utility-difference representation

- Paper anchor: Theorem 4.2, content/generalization.tex:35-44 and
  content/appendix-proofs.tex:20-124.
- Contract: every KLST* choice probability has an increasing
  utility-difference representation with the stated range and symmetry
  condition.
- Producers:
  reproduction/falsification.py::claim_4_proof_dependency_check,
  reproduction/representation_search.py::claim_4_exhaustive_search,
  reproduction/reduction_audit.py::claim_4_reduction_audit, and
  reproduction/adversarial_search.py::claim_4_adversarial_falsification.
- Evidence: .openresearch/artifacts/claim_4/,
  .openresearch/artifacts/claim_4_route_2/,
  .openresearch/artifacts/claim_4_route_3/,
  .openresearch/artifacts/claim_4_route_4/, and the Claim 4 row in
  space_candidate/artifacts/results.json.
- Route results: the published pointwise lemma has a 7/10 counterexample;
  the finite route exhausts 125 models, 9 lotteries, and 9^6 = 531441
  monotonicity sextuples per model, with 19 KLST* models and 19 represented;
  the reduction route finds an atomic/mixed-lottery domain gap; and the
  adversarial route tests 6,000 candidates across seeds 4401, 4402, and
  4403, finding 5,987 nonrepresentable candidates but zero
  axiom-satisfying nonrepresentable candidates.
- Status: **BLOCKED** with low confidence.
- Boundary: finite searches are falsification attempts, not a proof of a
  universal theorem. A complete proof must close the domain step, or a valid
  assumption-satisfying counterexample must be found.

## C5 — proper-loss triptych and DPO specialization

- Paper anchor: Theorem 4.4 and Corollary 4.5,
  content/generalization.tex:96-117 and
  content/appendix-proofs.tex:173-212.
- Contract: the canonical proper-loss identities hold for strictly proper
  binary losses, symmetry supplies the reflected identity, and symmetric
  log-loss with the sigmoid link recovers logistic DPO.
- Producer: reproduction/proof_certificates.py::claim_5_certificate.
- Evidence: .openresearch/artifacts/claim_5/raw.json,
  independent_checker_output.json, and negative_control_output.json.
- Result: subgradient and Fenchel residuals are 0; all three DPO residuals
  are 0; 19 asymmetric trials preserve the general identities while all 19
  reject the extra symmetry identity; the reversed-link control has residual 2.
- Status: **VERIFIED** for the stated canonical-link and DPO-specialization
  scope.
- Boundary: this is an algebraic reproduction, not a claim that all
  preference-training dynamics are identical.

## Overall interpretation

Claims 1 and 3 are falsified under explicit contracts; Claims 2 and 5 are
verified at their stated mathematical scope; Claim 4 remains unresolved. The
historical live judge score of 5/10 is preserved as provenance, not used as
scientific evidence. The forecasted 8–9/10 range in the research notes is not
a judge result.
