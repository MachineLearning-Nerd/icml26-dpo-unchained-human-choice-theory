# Assumption-satisfying falsification route

Claim 1 uses the theorem's own universal quantifiers: identity `psi`, sigmoid `F_tilde`, and the paper's real-valued closed-simplex loss definition. Properness at true probability zero contradicts the forced unbounded negative limit of `ell0`.

Claim 3 combines only expandability and bearability. Applying bearability to a mixed lottery compared with itself forces every atomic pair to have zero abstention. This contradicts the advertised ability of KLST* to allow abstention.

Claim 4 is not called false. A rational counterexample breaks a lemma in the supplied proof, but it does not establish a full KLST* counterexample to the representation theorem.
