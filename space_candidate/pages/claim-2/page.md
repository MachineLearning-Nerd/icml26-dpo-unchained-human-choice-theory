## Exact contract and assumptions

Theorem 4.1, v4 anchors `content/generalization.tex:23–29` and `content/appendix-proofs.tex:4–18`: if `R` is regret of a proper loss on `Delta_n`, then for all simplex points `p,q`, `R(p||q)` is the Bregman divergence generated by `phi(p)=−L(p,p)` with subgradient selection `G(q)=−ell(q)`. [Contract](../../artifacts/claim_contract.json); [source audit](../../artifacts/source_audit.md).

The checker assumes only properness, the loss/regret definition, and the specified subgradient convention. It does not select a sample count from a tested rate or restrict the proof to log or Brier losses.
